Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026

This policy covers shorts-publisher, the tool the owner of the Past Plot Twists YouTube channel uses to upload and schedule the channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

  • With the owner's consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel, set their titles, descriptions and release times, and read view statistics of those videos.
  • It works only with the owner's own channel. It does not access other users' accounts or data.

Storage and sharing

  • The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the owner's computer. It is never sent anywhere except to Google to get access tokens.
  • No personal data is collected from viewers or anyone else, and no data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
